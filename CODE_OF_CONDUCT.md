# Kode Etik / Code of Conduct

[English](#english) · **Bahasa Indonesia**

Proyek ini mengikuti [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/id/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Singkatnya:

- Bersikap ramah dan menghargai siapa pun, apa pun latar belakang, pengalaman, atau bahasanya.
- Kritik diarahkan ke kode dan gagasan, bukan ke orangnya.
- Pelecehan, hinaan, ancaman, dan membuka data pribadi orang lain tidak diterima di issue, diskusi, PR, maupun kanal lain proyek ini.

Pelanggaran bisa dilaporkan secara privat lewat [laporan keamanan GitHub](https://github.com/melkimahdali/zusantara-core/security/advisories/new) atau ke pemelihara (@melkimahdali). Laporan ditangani secara rahasia. Pemelihara boleh menghapus komentar, menutup diskusi, atau memblokir akun yang melanggar.

## English

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and respectful to everyone, whatever their background, experience, or language.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Harassment, insults, threats, and sharing other people's private information are not accepted in issues, discussions, PRs, or any other project space.

Report violations privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/melkimahdali/zusantara-core/security/advisories/new) or to the maintainer (@melkimahdali). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock discussions, or block accounts that violate this code.
